Masking every query to a band of $w$ preceding keys makes the cost $O(n \cdot w
\cdot d)$ rather than $O(n^2 d)$, and stacking $L$ such layers gives an effective
receptive field of $L \cdot w$ — information propagates, but only at one window
per layer.

What is not yet settled here, and the reason this is a stub, is how the band
interacts with the position scheme and with attention sinks: the first few tokens
appear to matter far more than the band alone would predict.
